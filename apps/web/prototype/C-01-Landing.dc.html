Programmable liberalism

Economic models, written as code. You choose which one to work under.

Three ideas

Rules are code

Every rule comes with a plain-language explanation and a link to the contract that enforces it.

Models compete

Treasuries sit side by side. The platform ranks none of them; you compare and choose.

Humans stay in control

Agents act, but owners approve every membership with a wallet signature.
